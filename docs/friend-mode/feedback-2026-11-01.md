# Feedback round, 2026-11-01

Eleven items from a deep co-op test session. All built on
`feature/friend-mode` the same day; untested in play unless marked.

1. **Cloak on load (Serpent Isle paperdolls).** Black Gate's cloak (285)
   is worn in the neck (amulet spot, filling the cloak spot) as its
   *alternate* ready type. Loading a game puts readied things back with
   `Actor::add_readied`, which set the neck flag only when neck was the
   *preferred* type, so a loaded cloak sat in the amulet spot unflagged
   and the paperdoll drew it as a plain item in a box until it was worn
   again. Fixed in `add_readied` (an Exult bug). Space's "Ready" also
   asked `add_readied` for the marker types (neck, both hands, heavy
   gloves, scabbard), which aren't spots; `Friend_free_spot` now gives the
   real spot.
2. **Guest skill range during the host's conversation or pause.** A range
   circle shown just before the game started waiting stayed on screen,
   frozen, because the Class clock stops then. Circles round people now
   hide while the game waits (fields on their spots stay), and bar clicks
   then are dropped (`Paint_circles`, `Friend_class_frame`).
3. **Controller rings on a guest during the host's conversation.** They
   don't open while the host talks or pauses, and one already open closes
   unused (`Controller_ring_cancel`).
4. **E lag with nothing in reach.** Exult's reach check (`is_grabable`)
   runs up to five path searches per thing, each failing slowly for one
   behind a wall. E now floods the walkable tiles near the player once
   (`Friend_walkable_area`) and runs the real check only on things with a
   walkable tile within grabbing distance.
5. **"Avatar only" on a guest's person hint.** Gone: only the actions
   there are.
6. **The Avatar starts talking: a guest's picks clear and its windows
   close** (the conversation's begin event).
7. **Guests use the abacus** (desk items 675, frame 11): it only says the
   party's gold, so it runs on the guest's copy.
8. **Abacus on the controller's utilities ring**, usable when anyone in
   the party carries one. Guests' ring has no Spellbook.
9. **Doors and the party.** Exult closed a door right behind each NPC
   whose walk opened it, shutting it on the next companion, and WASD
   closed it behind the player alone. Now doors the party opens wait in
   one list (gamewin.cc, `Friend_door_passed`) and close once no party
   member within 12 tiles is still on the side it was opened from or in
   the doorway: the last one through closes it. The dead, sleeping and
   paralyzed don't count; after 30 seconds the door is left open. Other
   people's doors close behind them as before.
10. **Sorting and placing in containers.** Sort laid things in rows as
    tall as their tallest thing, which left gaps and wrapped early. Now
    each thing (a stack of look-alikes fanned as one) goes in the first
    place where its picture touches nothing, top to bottom and left to
    right, and only overlaps where it overlaps least once nothing fits.
    New things dropped in are placed the same way (a crowded window used
    to fall back to Exult's pile at the top left).
11. **Research: who gets experience.** Only the killing blow. In
    `Actor::reduce_health`, when a creature is defeated (and wasn't
    asleep) its experience value is worked out from its stats, monster
    data and gear, halved; `Actor::attacked` then adds it to the attacker
    whose hit finished it. Damage before that earns nothing, and each
    companion levels from its own experience. For our skills: volleys
    (real shots) and Julia's powder keg (`attacked`) give it to the user;
    Katrina's trap ticks (`reduce_health` with no experience asked for)
    give none; a summoned creature's kills give it to the summon, so it
    is lost.

    **Decided and built, same day: shared experience** (Game menu → Class
    System → "Experience: Shared | Killing blow", Shared by default, the
    host's rule, shown even with skills off). A kill by the party goes to
    the Avatar and every party member alive, awake and within 20 tiles of
    it: the kill's value × (1 + 0.15 per member past the first), divided
    among them, the remainder to whoever made the kill. So healers and
    support companions keep up; everyone levels slower than a lone killer
    would (four sharing get about 36% each, seven about 27%), the bonus
    keeping the level-4 and level-7 skills within reach. Trap ticks and a
    summoned creature's kills now count as the party's (the summoner's,
    shared). Anyone else's kills, quest experience from the game's
    scripts, and "Killing blow" work as in Exult
    (`Friend_class_give_experience`, friendclass.cc, called from
    `Actor::attacked`).

## Test round, same day

Tested and passed: cloaks with Serpent Isle paperdolls, no skill range,
windows or picks on a guest when the Avatar starts talking, doors much
better, container layout better (to try again), shared experience. Item 2
had been seen on an older build. Then:

- **Take all button took nothing.** A window's button runs while Exult's
  click is still a drag (with no object), and `Game_window::move_object`
  refused to move anything during a drag. It now refuses only while an
  object is being dragged (`Dragging_info::has_object`).
- **A companion still shut in now and then, leaving a building.**
  Followers step after the leader (`Party_manager::step`) and never open
  doors; only a long walk's path does. So a follower behind a closed door
  with the leader just outside stood there. Now (host and single player)
  a follower that hasn't moved for 2 seconds, with a closed, unlocked
  door within 3 tiles and the Avatar or the played character on its other
  side within 16 tiles, gets the door opened; it closes again once the
  party is through (`Unstick_followers`, gamewin.cc).
  Correction from the user: the door was *open*, and the companion was
  trapped behind it (an open door's leaf boxes the corner by the
  doorway). So a follower that hasn't moved for 2 seconds within 2 tiles
  of an open door, with its leader more than 3 tiles off, gets a real
  path walk to the leader (`walk_path_to_tile`); with no path at all it
  is put on a free spot beside the leader.
  Still caught now and then (a screenshot: a companion in the pocket
  between an inward-swung leaf and the wall). Two misses: the distance was
  measured to the door's reference tile, one end of a 4-tile leaf, so a
  follower by the far end read as 4+ tiles off; and the test was standing
  perfectly still, while a trapped follower shuffles. Now: within 2 tiles
  of any tile of the door, and no nearer its leader for 2.5 seconds (the
  same for closed doors). Doors don't hinge on one side: an open door is a
  different shape (270 ↔ 376, 432 ↔ 433, the Abbey doors), placed by the
  door's script per shape and frame, so the pathfinder finds the way round
  rather than a rule per side.
  Better, but the user preferred single file: **built.** While any door
  (open or closed) is within 3 tiles of the leader or a follower, the
  formation (spots two tiles behind and to each side, which could land in
  the pocket) gives way to a line: each follower, in formation order,
  steps into the tile the one ahead of it just left
  (`Party_manager::single_file`, party.cc). Away from doors the formation
  comes back. The unstick checks stay as a safety net.
- **Controller ring on a browser guest:** the web build was older;
  rebuilt.
- **E still stuttered:** the red X. Exult's `Mouse::flash_shape` waits 0.6
  seconds (`SDL_Delay`), stopping the game. The Target System's keys (Q,
  E, Space, the backpack keys) no longer flash it; their notices say why.
- **Shared experience: 7 each, one got 8.** As designed: what doesn't
  divide evenly goes to whoever made the kill.
