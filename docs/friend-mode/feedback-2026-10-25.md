# Feedback round, 2026-10-25

Notes from play-testing that aren't about the controller (the controller's
own notes are in [the controller plan](controller-plan.md), "Pad test
notes"). Written down on `feature/controller` while testing; to be fixed on
`feature/friend-mode`.

## The Avatar knocked out: the companions can't act

**Fixed (2026-10-26):** the player's input asks whether the character
being played can act (`Game_window::player_can_act`, `_charmed`), not the
Avatar: Q, E, Space, walking (keys, WASD, the pad), the key bindings,
clicks and double-clicks. What is about the Avatar itself (the party
fighting on when it falls, schedules) still asks about the Avatar.
Untested in play; not decided: switching to a conscious companion by
itself when the Avatar falls.

**Seen:** the Avatar was knocked down (unconscious) in a fight. Switching
to any other party member (leading a companion) worked, but the led
companion couldn't attack or do anything.

**Likely cause (not yet checked in play):** the input paths ask whether the
*Avatar* can act, not the character being played. `main_actor_can_act()`
guards the Target System's Q, E and Space (`Friend_mode_cycle_target`,
`Friend_mode_space`, `Friend_mode_cycle_object` in exult.cc), every key
binding without `allow_if_cant_act` (`KeyBinder::DoAction`, keys.cc), and
the mouse in `Handle_event` (`avatar_can_act`). With the Avatar down, all
of them refuse, whoever is being led.

**To decide:** while leading a companion, check the led companion instead
(`gwin->get_controlled_actor()`), but keep the Avatar's state for what
truly needs the Avatar (the story, saving, the Avatar's own spells). Also
whether the game should switch to a conscious companion by itself when the
Avatar falls, and what a guest sees meanwhile.

## A backpack's window doesn't say whose it is

**Fixed (2026-10-26):** when a container in someone's inventory opens (by
key, pad or click), the line at the top says whose it is ("Iolo's
backpack", "Iolo's bag"), as Share / View does going round the windows
(`Friend_mode_name_new_windows`). A label on the window itself was tried
first and taken out.

**Seen (2026-10-26, while testing the controller):** with several party
members' backpacks open, a backpack window alone on screen gives no clue
whose it is; it's easy to lose track. (Paperdolls show their owner;
backpacks look alike.)

**To do on `feature/friend-mode`:** a title above a container window in
someone's inventory, "Iolo's backpack" (the owner's name and the
container's), in the game's small font, as the paperdoll names. Not
controller-specific, so it isn't on `feature/controller`.

## Q (people) should offer the Avatar too

**Fixed (2026-10-26):** Q offers the Avatar (never the one being played),
after everyone outside the party and before the companions
(`Friend_is_party_pick`, the sort in `Friend_mode_cycle_target`). A guest
gets the Avatar but no other companions, as before. With the Avatar
picked out of combat, the hint line is empty (no "Attack"). Oneself too
(potions, a healing pool): Q offers the character being played, in its
place in that order, and Space on oneself does nothing. In the crosshair
the party and oneself are offered in combat as well, and a pick there is
never attacked (`friend_crosshair_cycle`). Untested in play.

**Seen (2026-10-26, after the controller work):** the Target System's
people (Q, and L1 on the pad, in the world and in the crosshair) never
offer the Avatar, so a companion one leads can't pick the Avatar (to give
water from a pool, a heal, an item), and neither can a guest.

**To do on `feature/friend-mode`:** the Avatar joins the people Q cycles
through (never the character being played), in this order: everyone else
in range first (people and creatures outside the party), then the Avatar,
then the companions (as the party option allows). In
`Friend_mode_cycle_target` and whatever builds its list.

## Space and Shift+Space on people (2026-10-26)

After the targeting changes: "Attack (C, then Space)" made attacking a
two-step affair, and picking oneself or a companion had nothing useful on
Space. Now:

| Picked | Space | Shift+Space (L2+Cross) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oneself | View Character (one's paperdoll) | |
| A companion, or the Avatar | Talk, if they have something to say | View Character |
| A hostile one | Attack (combat mode comes on by itself) | Attack |
| Anyone else | Talk, if they talk | Attack (combat mode comes on by itself; a peaceful person asks "Again" first) |

A guest views only its own character, and talks only to merchants and
trainers, as before. In combat mode Space attacks as it did.
(`Friend_mode_space`, `Friend_space_hint`, `Friend_is_party_or_avatar`,
`Friend_may_view_character`.) Untested in play.
