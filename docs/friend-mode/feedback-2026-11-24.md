party formation as the original game does it (checked against the DOS
version in DOSBox), and softer night lighting (optional). On

Where it says **needs a log**, the cause isn't sure yet.

## A. Party formation: the original's shapes

**What the original does** (DOS version, avatar facing north, followers
behind; offsets in tiles, x to the right, y behind the leader):

| Followers | Spots |
|---|---|
| 1 | (+2, +2) |
| 2 | (-2, +2) (+2, +2) |
| 3 | (-2, +2) (+2, +2) · (0, +4) |
| 4 | (-2, +2) (+2, +2) · (-1, +4) (+1, +4) |
| 5 | (-2, +2) (+2, +2) · (-2, +4) (+2, +4) · (0, +6) |
| 6 | (-2, +2) (+2, +2) · (-1, +4) (+1, +4) · (-2, +6) (+2, +6) |
| 7 | (-2, +2) (+2, +2) · (-3, +4) (0, +4) (+3, +4) · (-2, +6) (+2, +6) |

Seven followers is the most (eight in the party with the Avatar), as in
Exult (`EXULT_PARTY_MAX` 8 counts the Avatar's slot too).

These are read off the screenshot (about 100 px a row, taken as 2 tiles).
Check them against DOSBox once it's built. The 4- and 6-follower inner
pairs are about half as wide as the outer ones.

- **Only four directions.** The shape turns only when the leader turns to
  N, E, S or W. Walking a diagonal, the formation keeps its facing and just
  slides along. Exult's way (`dir / 2` on every step, so a diagonal
  alternates N and E and the shape swings every step) is the wobble we've
  been fighting.
- **Exult's way now:** a fixed tree (`followers[]` in `party.cc`). Each
  member follows another member, 2 tiles behind and to one side, whatever
  the party's size, so a big party fans out into a wide triangle.

**Plan:** replace the tree with a table of spots per party size, rotated
for the facing. Each follower steps toward its own spot, measured from the
leader (not from the member ahead of it):
- The facing changes only on a straight N/E/S/W step. Diagonal steps keep
  it.
- Slots go by party order (the Shift+O order) among the members walking in
  formation, so the shape fits however many are actually following.
- Keep what works already: single file for doors, swamps and narrow
  passages (`single_file`), the mirror choice on a 180° turn (each keeps
  to its side), and gliding in time with the leader.
- Guests' companions (played by their guests) aren't in the shape. The
  rest close up around them. (Unless the original keeps a hole for them:
  nothing to compare against there.)

## Causes found, with the fix

1. **Guest: WASD and mouse walking stutters, the controller is smooth.**
   All three call `start_actor` every frame, at different speeds:
   - mouse: `avatar_speed`
   - WASD: `get_walking_speed`
   - pad: the slower of its push speed and the walking speed

   Likely cause: the keys and mouse walk the guest's companion faster than
   the host takes its steps. The guest then runs ahead, and the host's echo
   pulls it back (`guest_sent_steps` in `exult.cc`). The pad is slow enough
   not to. **Needs a log:** a local host/guest run, walking with WASD,
   comparing the guest's "requested move" lines with the host's echoes,
   plus `QOTC_GLIDE_LOG`.
   **Likely fix:** the guest walks no faster than the host's step rate.
   Leave an echo of a step it already took alone instead of snapping back.

2. **Co-op: changing the order (Shift+O) breaks walking. Companions go
   back and forth between their old and new spots, and stop or fall behind
   with nothing blocking them.** The cause is a mix-up in `party.cc`:
   - `move_followers` finds a follower with `valid[slot]`, where the slot
     is the party number.
   - `valid` is a compacted list. It leaves out the guest's companion,
     anyone asleep, paralysed or fighting, and anyone already walking.
   - So in co-op the two numberings disagree. One companion can be
     stepped twice on each of the leader's steps, toward two different
     spots (back and forth), and another is never stepped at all (left
     behind until the follow schedule notices it's more than 6 tiles off).
   - `choose_mirror` has the same mix-up.
   - Shift+O shows it because reordering moves the guest's companion to
     another slot.

   **Fix:** comes with A: one spot per member walking, looked up by the
   member itself, never through `valid`'s positions. Shift+O then just
   hands out the spots again.

3. **Guest: "Following the Avatar" flickers with a pack open and the more
   menu up while the Avatar walks.**
   - While one of Exult's own menus is open, the guest asks the host to
     drive its companion (`Friend_mode_modal_menu`), meant to be quiet.
   - But the host's answer turns on `friend_following`
     (`Friend_mode_apply_follow`), and that shows the bar.
   - It seems to toggle on and off as the menu opens and closes, or as
     the host answers.

   **Fix:** the host marks follows started by a menu, and the guest
   doesn't show the bar for them. **Check** in a log which side toggles it
   each step.

4. **Torches have no Ready.** Torches have a script (it lights them). In
   the item menu (`exult.cc` around 5450 and 11470), "has a script" comes
   before "can be worn" in the if/else chain, so a torch only ever offers
   Light. **Fix:** for light sources (torch 595, lit torch 701, and the
   like) offer both: Light/Snuff, and Ready/Put away.

5. **The mining machine can't be used through the Target System.** It's
   on E's usable list (shape 410). Its script (`ucread 19a`):
   - It looks for part 411 within 10 tiles and checks its own quality
     (1–3); otherwise it just plays an animation.
   - Otherwise it makes ore (shapes 914–916, blackrock among them).

   Reported by the guest: E never selects it. It's already in the usable
   group with the levers (so its kind isn't the problem, and it isn't
   decor). E's candidate filter (`exult.cc` around 5750) must be dropping
   it. Suspects, all measured from the machine's anchor tile, which on a
   big shape sits at its far corner, maybe inside its own footprint:
   - the reach distance;
   - the "walkable within 5" check;
   - `is_grabable`, the path check;
   - the roof check (`Friend_is_hidden_by_roof`). It looks 4 lifts above
     the thing's anchor tile, so a tall thing can find a beam or a ledge
     above part of its footprint. Lamps and lamp posts had this
     (2026-10-23, "reaches the roof, stands under its eave"), and tall
     creatures were their own roof (2026-10-28).

   **Plan:**
   - In the Minoc mine, a temporary log in E's loop for 410 prints which
     check fails (as was done for caltrops, feedback-2026-11-14).
   - Measure big fixtures from their nearest footprint tile.
   - Then a guest's use: 410 isn't in the guest's use list
     (`Friend_mode_guest_item_use`), so once selected it would say "The
     Avatar must do this". Its script only makes ore beside the machine
     (no story flags or windows), so add it as a fixture the guest may
     use.

6. **After a formation change, the party sometimes paths badly and spreads
   out.** Same cause as 2. Fixed with A.

7. **Switching between party members fills the screen with notices.** Each
   switch adds "Leading <name>" (`Friend_mode_notice`, `exult.cc:1093`).
   **Fix:** a new "Leading …" replaces the last one instead of stacking.

8. **Picking up a backpack, with a bag readied in that spot, does nothing,
   with no message.** The pickup tries to move the worn bag into the pack,
   fails because it won't fit, and stops without saying anything (the
   "No room" there only covers some cases). **Fix:** say why, as dragging
   does: show the "won't fit" cursor (`Mouse::wontfit`, as in `drag.cc`)
   and a "Won't fit" notice. Check the guest's refusal reasons use the same
   words.

9. **Make torches last longer.** The lit torch's script (`ucread 2bd`)
   burns down through its timer event. Find where the minutes left are set
   (fn 0839 / 0905, or the item's quality) and lengthen them. How long?
   **Agreed: double.**

10. **Stairs: the light jumps on every step.**
    - With Relight on, a carried torch is a lamp at the carrier's lift
      plus its height (`gamerend.cc`, "Torches and other lights the party
      carries").
    - On stairs the lift changes by a step at a time, so the lamp and its
      shadows jump each step.
    - Each stair tread also takes light and shadow as a floor of its own
      height.

    **Fix:**
    - The carried light's height follows the nearest real floor (the
      storey's base lift: 0, 5, 10…) or eases between steps, not each
      tread.
    - Stair shapes are lit as slopes between the two floors, not as
      separate ledges.

## B. Softer night light (optional)

The three hard cuts are the palette, as suspected. At night
`Game_clock::get_final_palette` (`gameclk.cc`) picks one of four palettes
from the light around the Avatar:
- night
- candle (under 224)
- single light (under 640)
- many lights (above that)

Each threshold crossed swaps the palette at once.

**Feasible:** blend between neighbouring palettes by how far the light
level is between the thresholds, or fade over about half a second whenever
the level changes. The blend could come from the same palette blending
dawn and dusk already use. Each new blend means re-sending the palette,
cheap at that size. With Relight on, its tables are rebuilt from the
palette when it changes (`rebuild_tables`): check that the cost is fine
mid-fade.

## As built

**A, 2 and 6: done, tested 2026-11-25** ("nearly flawless"; the user will
still compare with DOSBox). In `party.cc`:
- A table of spots per party size replaces the follower tree. Each
  follower steps toward its own spot, measured from the leader, front rows
  first, looked up by the member itself (the `valid` mix-up behind 2 is
  gone).
- The formation faces one of four ways. A diagonal step keeps the facing
  when it leans that way. A turn waits for 2 straight steps the new way
  (`formation_turn_steps`), so a WASD key tapped on the way into a
  diagonal doesn't swing the party round.
- A follower 3 or more tiles from a spot moving away from it (or to the
  side) walks a path there at 1.5 times the leader's speed, aimed a little
  ahead, 4 tiles at a time. One whose spot comes toward it (the party
  turned around) steps to meet it: a path went to where the spot had been,
  and the last in line walked off the other way.
- `Actor::follow` leaves formation walkers alone while the leader moves (a
  finished catch-up walk set off for the leader's destination), and sends
  one far off to its spot when the leader stops (it went somewhere near
  the leader at random).
- Guests' companions are left out of the shape while their guests play
  them, and counted while they follow.
- `QOTC_PARTY_LOG=<file>` now logs each follower per step: where it is,
  its spot, how far off, and step or path.

## C. Keyring (added 2026-11-25)

The Keyring mod's keyring (shape 1100, its picture and data), without the
mod's quest: sold by the four lockpick sellers, with one party-wide list
of keys.
- Picture: `data/bg/keyring.shp` packed as `bg/qotc_shapes.vga` (a shapes
  flex with only 1100) into `exult_bg.flx`, loaded between the game's
  `shapes.vga` and the patch's (`Shapes_vga_file::init`). Its info (the
  mod's TFA, weight 1) is set in `read_info` when nothing else did, and
  its name in `Game::setup_text`. Not in Exult Studio (editing).
- Keys: Exult's Keyring (Serpent Isle's, `keyring.dat`), now read, written
  and saved for Black Gate too. Inn keys (quality 255) and Alagner's (254)
  are refused, as in the mod. The keyring's frame shows how many (0 to 4).
- Adding: a key dropped on a keyring (`Game_object::drop`), or "Add keys"
  in its item menu (the party's keys; a guest's own). A key's own item menu
  has "Add to keyring" while the party (a guest: its companion) carries one. "Remove keys"
  (host and single player) puts them back in the pack (those that fit;
  the rest stay on the ring).
- Using: K and E's unlock (`Try_keys_on`, `Friend_lock_of`) and a
  double-click (as K) try the ring after real keys. The ring opens a lock
  by making its key for a moment and running the key's own script.
- Guests: the host sends the list (`Blob_kind::keyring`, protocol 43); a
  key a guest drops on its keyring is put on by the host, which holds it.
- Shops: `Usecode_internal::friend_shops_sell_keyrings` adds a keyring
  entry to 0x85A (Budo, both price lists), 0x874 (Dell), 0x899 (Greg)
  and 0x8A1 (Jehanne) as their script loads: each list gets an item
  before "nothing", copied from the backpack's (Budo: the torch's), and
  the jumps across are moved. A shop not as expected is left alone. Price
  20 gold. A patch folder's usecode still replaces them.
