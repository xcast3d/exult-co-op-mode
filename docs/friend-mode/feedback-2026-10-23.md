## Batch G: labels, keys and the pick (built and tested 2026-10-23)

Built as planned below, with these details: the reading order is
`Friend_window_reading_order` (8 pixel rows; paperdolls keep their old
order), and E starts again in windows once the newest window
(`Friend_newest_window`) isn't the one the pick was made under. The open
pick is `Friend_mode_pick_when_open`, checked each frame for 2 seconds by
`Friend_mode_check_open_pick`, only for the box group opened by Space.
Escape is `Friend_mode_escape_clears_pick` in `Handle_event` (no
modifiers). After testing: a pick inside a window is cleared and the
windows close in the same press. A guest's
Shift+click on a face goes through `Friend_mode_guest_request_companion`,
which Shift+Tab now shares (`friend_cycle_wanted`).

The plan as written:

**G1. The guest's hold button says "Stop", not "Take over".** "Take over"
didn't fit the 46-pixel button and showed as "ake ove". The label comes
from `Friend_mode_hold_button_label` in exult.cc (~5171): "Follow" / "Stop"
on a guest; the hover notice stays "Take over your companion" with
"Shortcut: Y". The width comment in friendmap.cc (`button_w`) is updated;
the width can stay.

**G2. E in a window always starts at the top left.** Two causes:

- The pick is kept between windows. `Friend_mode_cycle_object` never
  starts again inside windows (`moved` is false there), so a pick left
  over from before (the first item, taken or not, in a window closed and
  opened again) makes the next E go to the second. Fix: remember the
  window the pick was made in (the container, and the newest window then);
  when the newest window is another one, or was opened since, start again
  at the first.
- The order is by the items' positions (`get_ty`, then `get_tx`), which
  are their shapes' hot spots, not where they show. An item one pixel
  higher in the second column comes first, and a tall one sorts lower
  than its top. Fix: sort by where each shape is drawn
  (`Gump::get_shape_rect`), in rows: items whose tops are within about 8
  pixels of each other are one row, left to right; rows top to bottom.
  `Friend_window_index` and `Friend_mode_pick_next_in_window` use the
  same list, so taking with Space goes on in the same order.

**G3. Opening a container picks its first item.** Space on a chest, body
or bag on the map opens it, then picks the top left item in its window,
so taking everything is: E to the chest, Space, Shift+Space. A pending
"pick the first item once a window for this container shows" is checked
each frame for a second or two (a guest's window may take a moment). An
empty container says "Empty" and keeps no pick. Only for an open by
Space, not a double-click or Shift+1-8 (opening one's own pack shouldn't
move the pick).

**G4. Escape clears the target first.** Escape is Exult's
`close_or_menu` (keyactions.cc): close the windows, or else open the game
menu. Now, before that, in `Handle_event`: if there is a pick (Q's or
E's), Escape clears it ("No target") and stops an attack as clearing does
(`Friend_mode_clear_target`). Next Escape: windows, then the menu. Chat
keeps its own Escape. Recommended: with G3 a chest's window always has a
pick, so closing it takes two presses; a pick inside a window could
instead be cleared with the window in one press. Decide when testing.

**G5. Shift+click on a face takes that companion, on a guest.**
`Friend_mode_shift_click_face` returns early on a guest (it was written for
the host leading a companion). A guest now asks the host for the free
list, as Shift+Tab does (`friend_cycle_requested`), but with the wanted
companion (`friend_cycle_wanted`, -1 for "the next"): if it is free, claim
it; else "Iolo is another player's" / "Iolo isn't free". Its own face does
nothing. The Avatar's face does nothing on a guest.

## Batch H: attacking, running, the status bar (built and tested 2026-10-23)

Built: **H2** as decided below: `Friend_running` (exult.h,
`config/gameplay/running`), toggled by R in `Handle_event` before the key
bindings, read by `get_walking_speed`; the Caps Lock watch is gone; both
games' defaultkeys.txt bind `face_stats` to Shift-R. **H3:** the bar on
the left or right moves in by the width of Exult's column on that side
(`Face_stats::hud_part_rect`, part 0 left, 1 right; both are drawn flush
with the screen's edge at the HUD's scale, as the bar is). The corners are
left as they were: the columns are four faces tall and centred, so they
rarely reach them. **H1, found on retesting:** the testers pressed Space on
a rabbit out of combat, which talks, and a guest's talk to anyone but a
merchant says "The Avatar must do this". Now Space out of combat does
nothing on someone with no conversation (no NPC number or no script of
their own: deer, rabbits, rats; `Friend_has_conversation`, shared with the
hint line, which says "Attack (C, then Space)"), for everyone. C, then
Space attacks as before.


**H1. A guest's attack on a peaceful pick fails from peace.** Seen in the
live test: Q on a rabbit out of combat, C, Space: no attack. Already in
combat mode, Q on a rabbit then Space works. So it is the change from peace
to combat, not the attack itself. Out of combat Space talks, as designed
("The Avatar must do this" for a guest), so the plain attack by Space from
peace isn't wanted. To find, with both logs (`Friend Mode: guest attacks`,
`refused guest attack`, `attacks ... for guest`):

- the guest: C runs `toggle_combat`, which on a guest only calls
  `Friend_mode_combat_started` (it attacks hostile picks only); check the
  first Space then asks "Again to attack" and the second sends the attack,
  and that nothing (the target notice, the confirmation's timer, the pick
  being re-made) resets the confirmation in between;
- the host: whether the companion's combat schedule holds while the host
  itself is out of combat mode (in the working case someone was already
  fighting), or is turned back to following, waiting or the guest's
  stand (`Friend_mode_check_guest_attack`, `Combat_schedule`'s end for
  party members with no hostiles about).

The led companion (host, Shift+Tab) gets the same check.

**H2. Run and walk on R, not Caps Lock.** Caps Lock also capitalises chat,
so players turn it off to chat and on again after. Keys within reach of
WASD:

| Key | Today | As a run key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R | status bar style (`face_stats`) | toggle; best: "R for run", next to E and F |
| ` (backtick) | nothing | toggle; free, but awkward and moves on other layouts |
| X | spectators: watch the next player | free for players, but spectators use it |
| Ctrl (held) | Ctrl+S quicksave, Ctrl+D delete (cheat); Ctrl+click is a right-click on the Mac | no |
| Alt (held) | Alt+W wizard mode, Alt+S skins; Alt means "how many" | no |
| Shift (held) | dropped 2026-10-22: means too much | no |

Decided: **R toggles run and walk** ("Running" / "Walking"), and the
status bar style moves to Shift+R (it is also in the menu, and a player
runs far more often than changing it). The state is each machine's own, kept
in the config (`config/gameplay/running`) so it lasts across games.
`get_walking_speed` (keyactions.cc ~645) reads it instead of
`SDL_KMOD_CAPS`; the Caps Lock watch in the main loop (exult.cc ~9306)
goes. Guests run as before (their steps were already tested running).

**H3. Skill bar beside the vertical status bar.** With Exult's status bar
in its side columns (`Face_stats` mode 3, four faces down each side, centred)
and the skill bar on the left or right (`friend_bar_left_center`,
`friend_bar_right_center`), the bar sits on the faces. `Friend_class_bar`'s
placement (friendclass.cc ~2838) only avoids a bottom row. Fix: in mode 3,
move the bar inward past the column on its side (the column's width plus
the usual 3 pixels), and do the same for the four corners when the column
reaches them vertically. `Face_stats` needs to give each column's rect
(left and right), not only the whole region.

## Batch I: the F9 test kit (built and tested 2026-10-23)

Built as planned below: `Friend_mode_gather_companions(second_group)`;
what a player has is `Friend_test_kit_player_has` (a guest's companion,
one held for a rejoin, the one led), whose schedule and place are left
alone. Sending away mirrors the `remove_from_party` intrinsic (alignment
neutral), then the wait schedule. F8 takes no modifiers.


**I1. Gathering leaves players' companions alone.**
`Friend_mode_gather_companions` sets every companion's schedule to follow
the Avatar (or combat), including one a guest plays, whose host side then
fights the guest's input until the guest switches. Fix: skip the schedule
(and the move next to the Avatar) for a guest's companion
(`gwin->is_remote_friend`), one held for a rejoin, and the one the host
leads; they still get the weapons and level 7.

**I2. Two smaller groups, F9 and F8.** The kit asked for all nine and
Exult's party takes 8 besides the Avatar, so it brought in 8 (one more
than U7's seven) and Tseramed, last in the list, never came. Now:

- **F9:** Iolo, Shamino, Dupre, Jaana, Spark.
- **F8:** Sentri, Julia, Katrina, Tseramed.

Each key first sends the other group's companions out of the party
(`Party_manager::remove_from_party`, then waiting where they stand),
except anyone a player plays, holds for a rejoin or leads, then gathers
its own. Never more than seven companions: past that, "left out: the
party is full". F8 is free in Exult's keys (data/bg/defaultkeys.txt);
host only, as F9. Ctrl+F9 and Shift+F9 stay.
