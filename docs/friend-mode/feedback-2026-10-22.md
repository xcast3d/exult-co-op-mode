# Feedback round, 2026-10-22

The user's notes after playing the Class System and leading a companion.
Several walk back earlier choices (badges under people, Space and Shift+Space
in windows, Shift+S, unlock levels). Batches A and B are built and
tested (2026-10-22), and so are C and D; E, F and the fixes after them
are tested on the host (leading a companion included); the guest's side
is still to test.

Grouped into six batches, smallest and safest first. Each batch is one
commit (or a few), tested before the next. None of them needs a protocol
change except where noted.

## Batch A: numbers and art (built and tested 2026-10-22)

**A1. Unlock levels 1, 4, 7** (was 1, 3, 6). Iolo and the Avatar start at
level 3, so the second skill was open from the start. Built: two constants,
`level_second` and `level_third`, beside `Classes()` (friendclass.cc). Level
7 needs 3200 experience (U7: 1 + log2(exp / 50)). The F9 test kit now
raises companions to level 7.

**A2. Iolo: Song of Peace first, Song of Warding second.** Built: Song of
Peace level 1 and 30 s, Song of Warding level 4 and 60 s. The icon files
are in `Classes()` order, so the two pictures were swapped in
`scratch/class-skill-icons/manifest.json` and rebuilt (the old manifest is
kept as `manifest.before-iolo-swap.json`).

**A3. Sentri's short-range strike.** Master's Strike (`aim(G::pick, 1)`)
only worked with the pick beside him, and U7's melee AI often stands a tile
or more off, so it was refused most of the time. Built: a lunge, range 4
with `.dashing()` as Blade Dance, so Sentri darts beside the pick and
strikes. (The dash path counts its strike itself, so the "needs a bow"
message never comes up for it.) Riposte (his level 1, a stance) is
unaffected.

**A4. Class skill icons at 36x36** (they look large on screen). Built:
`build.py --size 36 --resample box`, copied to `data/friend/skills/`,
`skill_icon_size = 36`. They are
drawn at Exult's HUD scale (`Gump::is_hud`), so on screen they are 40 times
that scale; 36 is 10% smaller. If that isn't enough, 32 is the other size
the originals were made for. Don't scale at run time: the icons are 8-bit
palette pictures, and scaling them in the game (nearest neighbour) would
drop or double lines unevenly. Rebuild them from the full-size originals
kept in `scratch/class-skill-icons/originals/`:
`python build.py --size 36 --resample box`, copy the two `.shp` files to
`data/friend/skills/`, and set `skill_icon_size = 36` (friendclass.cc
2515). Check the bar's spacing and the tooltip position after.

**A5. All skills wait 5 seconds after any skill** (new, 2026-10-22), so
the Class AI or a guest with a high level can't fire all three at once and
clear a wave. Each skill's own cooldown still runs too. There was already a
1.5 s wait (`global_wait_ms`); built: raised to 5 s, and the bar now counts
it down on every slot (`Global_wait_left`), "All skills: 3 seconds" in the
tooltip. A constant for now; may go higher after play.

## Batch B: what draws where (built and tested 2026-10-22)

**B1. No status icons under people; on the status bar instead.** Built:
the marks are worked out each frame (`Update_marks`), for party members
only, and `Portrait_button::paint` draws them after U7's badges
(`Friend_class_status_marks`, `Friend_class_paint_status_mark`). U7's own
protection badge stands for protection, so the Class System no longer adds
a shield for it. Tested: all pass. After testing, the armour shield
(Quick Fix, Shield Ally, Riposte) is blue, not white, so it isn't read as
U7's protected badge, and the badges line up in U7's badge row (they had
been drawn from their top left, U7's from a hot spot, so they sat lower,
on the health bar). Plan as written: The
pictures under people (`Paint_marks`, friendclass.cc ~3180) go. The same
marks (up arrow, shield, down arrow) are drawn as badges on the party's
faces in Exult's status bar, next to U7's own (asleep, poisoned,
protected...: `Portrait_button::paint` in gumps/Face_stats.cc). The host
already sends the marks to guests (`Send_marks`), so guests' status bars
get them with no protocol change. Three small badge pictures are needed,
in the style of Exult's `fs_badges` (built in code, as the arrows are, or
added to exult.flx).

Foes have no face. Decided: a marked, weakened, calmed or stunned foe gets
only the words over its head ("*marked*", "*crippled*", "*stunned*"), no
picture. Check every skill that touches a foe has its words (Hunter's Mark
may have none yet).

The skill range circles stay (they show only while a skill acts, and are
an option).

**B2. Target arrow over windows.** Built: on a thing in a window the
arrow's layer sits at (1 << 18) + (1 << 16) - 1, over every ordinary
window, under books, signs and dialogs; on the world it goes back under
windows. Plan as written: The arrow was moved under every window
(z just below 1 << 18) so backpacks cover it in the world. For a pick
inside a window that hides it entirely. Fix: when the pick is in a window
(`friend_object_in_window`), raise the arrow's layer above that window
(`layer_set_z` to the window's own z plus one, or the top of the gump
range); back below windows when the pick is in the world.

**B3. Effect preview crash at sprite 20.** Found and fixed: Black Gate's
sprites 20 and 25 are empty (no frames), and a sprite effect's first step
looked up a frame that wasn't there. `Sprites_effect::handle_event` now
ends an empty one at once (any caller, not only the preview), and the
preview says "Sprite 20 is empty" instead of playing it. Plan as written: "Friend Class: preview sprite
20" then a segmentation fault. Reproduce under lldb (`lldb ./exult`,
Class System page, Effect preview, step to 20, Play), read the stack, and
fix the cause. Likely a sprite with no frames or an empty frame:
`Sprites_effect` reads `sprite.get_shape()` without a check. The preview
also passes reps 0; check what that means for a sprite with one frame. Then
step through every sprite to the end to catch the others.

## Batch C: keys (built and tested 2026-10-22)

Built as planned below, with these details: the list of things Space uses
on the map is `Friend_is_usable_thing` (readables, the gavel (desk item 675
frame 0), the bucket (810), the guests' usable and local shapes, never
gold); take all is one function, `Friend_mode_take_all`, shared with the
window button; Space no longer ignores Alt. Walking: `get_walking_speed`
(keyactions.cc) uses the medium speed unless Caps Lock is on; the main loop
says "Running" or "Walking" when Caps Lock changes. Shift+S with WASD does
nothing now; Shift+W still shows the watch.

**C1. Space and Shift+Space, one rule for each place.** Replaces the table
in the brief's Phase 9 ("Things, not just containers") and the hint line
(`Friend_space_hint`).

| Where | Space | Shift+Space | Alt+Space |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A person | talk (in combat: attack) | | |
| On the map: something with a use (gavel, bucket, fountain, books, scrolls, the cloth map, signs) | use or read | take (if it can be taken) | |
| On the map: a container or body | open | pick up (bags, baskets) | |
| On the map: doors, lamps, levers, furniture | use | pick up (if it can be) | |
| On the map: anything else | take | | |
| In someone else's window (a chest, a body) | take, books too | take all | split a stack (how many) |
| In one's own inventory | use, read, ready or put away; else drop after asking | drop | drop some (how many) |

What changes:

- On the map, things with a use are used, not taken. "Has a use" is a
  list, not every script: food or a gem lying on a merchant's table must
  not be eaten or "used". The list is the readables, the guests' usable
  and local shapes, plus gavel, bucket and fountains (C1 needs their shape
  numbers; see E1, E2).
- In a window, Shift+Space always takes everything (asking once if some
  of it isn't yours), even on a stack or a book. Splitting moves to
  Alt+Space. Books in a window are taken with Space, no longer read; they
  are read from one's own pack, or on the map.
- In one's own inventory "drop some" moves from Shift+Space to Alt+Space,
  so Alt always means "how many".
- Check Alt+Space isn't bound in Exult's keys, and on the Mac that Option
  arrives as Alt in SDL.

**C2. Walk, run, and no more Shift+S.**

- Walking (WASD and the arrow keys) uses the second fastest speed
  (`Mouse::medium_speed_factor`, 200) instead of the fastest (400). Combat
  keeps U7's slower combat speed.
- Decided: no Shift to run. Shift already means too much: Shift+Q, Shift+E,
  Shift+Space, Shift+Tab, Shift+I, Shift+C, Shift+1-8, Shift+click on a
  face, Shift+W and Shift+S with WASD, and Exult's own Shift+arrows (walk
  slower), Shift+H, Shift+V, Shift+F3.
- **Caps Lock toggles run and walk.** Exult binds nothing to it. Read
  `SDL_KMOD_CAPS` each frame, so nothing gets stuck. A notice ("Running" /
  "Walking") when it changes. Caps Lock also makes chat capitals, which is
  harmless.
- Shift+S no longer opens Save/Restore (Ctrl+S quicksaves, the game menu
  has Save). Shift+W, the watch, can stay, since Shift doesn't run.
- Guests: check the host accepts a guest's running steps (moves carry a
  step number and queue up to 3 tiles apart; running could trip that).

## Batch D: buttons on windows and the map (built and tested 2026-10-22)

Built: `Icon_button` in gumps/Gump.cc (Exult's drawn 16x16 button with a
9x9 picture): Sort on every container window (and Shift+C on any), Take all
beside it on containers in the world. The hold button is a layer in
friendmap.cc ("Hold" / "Follow" on the host, "Follow" / "Take over" on a
guest), shared with Y (`Friend_mode_hold_or_follow`); hovering says what
it does with "Shortcut: Y" under it. The skill bar's top-right place goes
under both (`Friend_map_top_right_height`).

**D1. Sort as a small icon, on every container.** The "Sort" text button
becomes a 16x16 icon (a picture of stacked rows) in the same spot below
the checkmark, and shows on chests, crates and bodies too, not only packs
(`Gump::is_inventory_container`, gumps/Gump.cc ~435 and the add at ~815).
`sort_objects` must allow world containers. To check first: a guest
sorting a chest changes only where things lie in the window; item sync
fingerprints containers, so either the host's layout comes back and undoes
it, or the sort must be sent to the host as a request. Decided: sorting world containers matters (a
ship's hold, chests on the magic carpet), and players overriding each
other's layout is fine. So a guest's sort should reach the host (or simply
be redone by the host's sync); no protection against two people sorting.

**D2. Take all as an icon next to it**, on containers in the world (chests,
crates, barrels, bodies, a bag on the ground), never on one's own pack or
a bag in any party member's inventory. Same as Shift+Space in that window
(C1), so one function serves both, with the same "Not all yours" question.
Guests' moves already go to the host one by one.

**D3. Hold / follow button under the minimap**, an alternative to Y. Two
states: "Hold" / "Follow" on the host and in single player, "Follow the
Avatar" / "Take over" on a guest. Hovering shows the two-line notice we
use for the Space hint: "Hold the party" and "(Y)". The minimap is an
option, off by default, and hides in dungeons, so the button needs a home
without it. Decided: under the minimap when it shows, else in the
top-right corner itself.

**D4. Minimap and enemy health bars to Game Display** (added
2026-10-22). Both moved from Game Engine to the end of Game Display, and
with the minimap on a "Minimap size" row: Small (as before) or Large, half
as big again on screen (the same area, drawn bigger;
`config/gameplay/minimap_size`). Built and tested.

## Batch F: conversation choices by key (built 2026-10-22, untested)

Built as proposed in the findings below, and decided: E always moves the
highlight and no longer picks the 14th choice (lists that long are rare,
and nobody counts past 9). `Conversation::move_highlight` and the white
highlight in `show_avatar_choices`; E, Shift+E and Space in
`get_user_choice_num` (host, single player, and a guest's own merchant
conversation); on a guest watching the Avatar's conversation, at the top of
`Handle_event`, Space sends the highlighted choice as a suggestion.

**F1. Conversation choices by key.** E (and Shift+E) moves a highlight
through the conversation's choices, Space picks it. Today choices take
1-9 and a-z. To find out: where Exult draws and hit-tests the choices
(conversation.cc), whether a highlight fits its painting, and how it sits
with a guest's "Suggest a reply" (E and Space would suggest on a guest).
It is also the controller plan's "choices by highlight"
([controller support](controller-plan.md)). Write up findings before
building.

### F1 findings (2026-10-22)

How choices work today:

- The Avatar's choices are read in `Usecode_internal::get_user_choice_num`
  (usecode/ucinternal.cc): a loop around `Get_click` (exult.cc) that takes
  a click on a choice (`Conversation::conversation_choice`) or a key, '1'
  to '9' then 'a' to 'z' for the 1st to 35th choice; Escape picks "bye".
  The same loop runs a guest's own merchant conversation.
- Choices are drawn in `Conversation::show_avatar_choices`
  (usecode/conversation.cc) in two passes, backgrounds then text, each
  with a round bullet (font character 127). Suggest a reply already
  recolors one choice in a player's colour there (`suggestions`), so a
  highlight can be drawn the same way.
- A guest watching the Avatar's conversation is not in that loop: its main
  loop runs, paused (`is_network_dialogue_paused`), and a click on a choice
  sends a suggestion (exult.cc, mouse button up).

Proposed, small (about 100 lines, no protocol change):

- `Conversation` keeps a highlighted choice (none at first, cleared with
  each new set of choices) and draws it in white, or with an arrow in
  place of its bullet; a suggestion's colour still shows on its own
  choice.
- Host and single player, in `get_user_choice_num`: E moves the highlight
  to the next choice, Shift+E the previous (round the end), Space picks the
  highlighted one. Space does nothing there today (it isn't a letter or a
  digit). E is also the key for the 14th choice ('1'-'9', then 'a'-'e'):
  E would stop picking it, which only matters in the longest lists, where
  the mouse still works. Decision needed: accept that, or skip E as a
  choice key only while a highlight is showing.
- A guest during the Avatar's conversation: E and Shift+E move its own
  highlight (in the paused main loop, where E would otherwise cycle
  things), Space sends it as a suggestion.
- A guest's merchant conversation runs in `get_user_choice_num`, so it gets
  the host's behaviour for free.
- The gamepad plan's "conversation choices by highlight"
  ([controller support](controller-plan.md)) would use the same highlight.

## After testing E and F (2026-10-22; host tested, guest not yet)

1. **The bucket was used by the Avatar**, for a guest and for a led
   companion. Its script animates `get_npc_object(-356)` (always the
   Avatar), and does it after walking, in a second call made when the walk
   ends, by which time the companion standing in had been forgotten. Now
   `Usecode_internal::get_item(-356)` gives the companion while one stands
   in (`Friend_mode_usecode_walker`, only during the allowed item, door
   and lever scripts run for a guest or a led companion), the walk keeps it
   for the call at the end (`Usecode_actor_action`'s walker), and the led
   companion's item use sets it too.
2. **The gavel said nothing** for a guest: the host refused every target
   ("target out of reach", the pick-up reach). U7 sets no reach on what
   the gavel strikes; now anything within 8 tiles of the companion.
3. **Shift+Tab on a guest** cycles to the next free companion: it asks the
   host for its list (its own and the free ones, never the Avatar, another
   player's, the host's led one or one held for a rejoin) and claims the
   next after its own.
4. **Who plays whom on the status bar:** a dot in each player's minimap
   colour on the face of the character they play (the host's: the Avatar or
   the companion it leads), on every machine, during a co-op session
   (`Friend_mode_controller_color`, `Portrait_button::paint`).

5. **The minimap drew over conversations.** It and the hold button are now
   at layer depth -3: over the world, under a conversation's two layers (-1
   and 0) and under windows.
6. **Talking to someone inside a building from outside.** Q picked people
   whose roof hid them, and Space started a conversation. Now Q and E skip
   anyone or anything under a roof that is being drawn (indoors while we
   are out, or in another building: `Friend_is_hidden_by_roof`, the same
   roof test Exult uses to decide which roofs to hide), and Space on a
   pick that has gone indoors says "Can't see Finnigan". Not in dungeons,
   whose ceilings work differently.

7. **The bucket, second try.** Leading a companion, it picked the bucket
   up, but after the click on the well the Avatar took over and filled it.
   The bucket's first step schedules its second (`execute_usecode_array`
   on itself, event 2), which asks for the well and walks there; a
   scheduled script forgot the companion, and the lead's safety rule
   handed control back when a script moved the companion. Now a
   `Usecode_script` keeps the companion it was made for and runs as it;
   the safety rule ignores scripts made for the companion itself
   (`Friend_story_controls` in gamewin.cc), still ending the lead for a
   story scene. For a guest the bucket asks for its target first, on the
   guest's screen (`on_anything`); the host keeps a target its script
   didn't use at once for that companion's next click, for 10 seconds
   (`Friend_mode_walker_click`), and a script running for a guest's
   companion never asks the host to click.
8. **The player dot** sits further right, beside the face rather than on
   the hair.
