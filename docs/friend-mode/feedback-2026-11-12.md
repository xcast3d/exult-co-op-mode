## Causes found, with the fix

1. **Guest can't play the harpsichord, the horn and the like.** Their
   scripts are one line each: `play_music` with a tune (harpsichord 689
   0x2B1, xylophone 690, harp 745: tunes 57, 56, 58). None of them is on
   `Friend_mode_guest_usable_shape`, so a guest gets "The Avatar must do
   this". Music is already the host's: whatever the host plays goes to every
   guest (`Friend_mode_note_music`), so a guest playing on its own copy
   would be heard by nobody else.
   **Fix:** add 689, 690, 745 to `Friend_mode_guest_usable_shape`: the host
   plays it for the guest and everyone hears it. Space says **Play**.
   - The horn (888, 0x378) is the ferry's: frame 0 plays a tune (24), frame
     1 plays one (48) and, by the right shore, calls the Ferryman with the
     camera on him (`set_camera`, the barge). **To decide:** let a guest blow
     frame 1 too (the ferry is world state, the host's anyway; the camera
     jump would be on the host's screen), or keep frame 1 the Avatar's.
     Decided: allowed (by double-click and the Target System), with
     `set_camera` skipped when the host runs a script for a guest.

2. **Whistle, lyre, lute not offered by the Target System.** They are in
   Loot (691-693), so E takes them and Space has no Play; carried, Space
   says Use but a guest's use isn't on any list. Lyre (0x2B3) and lute
   (0x2B4) are again just `play_music`; the whistle (693, 0x2B5) plays one,
   and frame 1 also closes windows and sets the schedule of every wisp
   (534) nearby: a story thing.
   **Fix:**
   - Move 691-693 from Loot to Usable: on the map Space **Play**, Alt+Space
     takes (as the bucket). In a pack Space says Play, Drop on Shift.
   - Guest list: 691, 692 and the whistle's frame 0 as "use on self" in
     `Friend_mode_guest_item_use`, so the host plays it for them (heard by
     all). The whistle's frame 1 stays the Avatar's.

3. **Class skills without the class item.** It's all there but unset:
   `Has_class_item` checks `Companion_class::item_shape`, which is -1 for
   every companion, and `Friend_class_require_items` (option "require
   items") defaults to off. The old plan (companion-skills.md, "Class
   items") was a special marked item placed in each home; this round asks
   for something simpler: the right *kind* of thing in the pack or hands.
   Decided 2026-11-12: only some companions need one. Iolo a lute (not the
   lyre), Spark a sling, Shamino and Tseramed a bow; Dupre's Shield Ally
   needs a shield (as now). Julia's hammer unsure: if anything, carried, not
   readied, or she is no use in a fight. The rest need nothing.
   **Fix:** `item_shape` becomes a short list of shapes per companion, any
   quality, readied or carried, and the option defaults to on. The bar
   already greys with "Needs ...", so the names change to the kind ("Needs
   a lute"). **To decide**, per companion:

   | Companion | Needs | Shapes |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Iolo | a lute (or a lyre?) | 692 (691) |
   | Shamino | a bow | 597, 606 magic bow (crossbows 598, 647 too?) |
   | Spark | a sling | 474 |
   | Tseramed | a bow | as Shamino |
   | Dupre | a shield (already what Shield skills check: `Has_shield`) | anything with armour in the off hand |
   | Sentri | a sword | 599, 602, 547, 551, 567, 604, 635, 637, 707 |
   | Julia | a hammer | 623 |
   | Jaana, Katrina | ? (Black Gate has no plain staff or crook shape) | none: no item needed |

   To check before turning it on: what each one starts with in a new game
   (Iolo's crossbow, Shamino's bow, Spark's start in Trinsic), so nobody
   starts with a grey bar unless that's wanted. A host change of the option
   already reaches guests (class state blob).

4. **The "mysterious craft" (267) isn't offered.** Its script (0x10B) only
   plays tune 39 (the easter egg's music). It is in no group, so it's Decor
   (off) and E skips it; a guest double-click gets "The Avatar must do
   this".
   **Fix:** 267 into Usable, Space says **Inspect**; add it to
   `Friend_mode_guest_usable_shape` so the host plays it for a guest and
   everyone hears it.

5. **A guest can't sit on a chair.** The chair (873, 0x369 then fn 080A)
   sits the Avatar (-356) in the clicked chair and then **every party member
   not waiting** in the chairs nearby (`get_party_list`, `sit_down`). Not on
   any guest list, so "The Avatar must do this". Run as is for a guest, our
   -356 substitution would sit the guest's companion, but the loop would sit
   the whole party too.
   **Fix:**
   - Guest double-click or Space on a chair (Space says **Sit**): the host
     checks the chair is free and within the guest's reach and sits only the
     guest's companion (`Sit_schedule`, as `sit_down` does), not the script.
   - When the Avatar sits, the script's loop skips companions a guest plays
     (so they aren't pulled into chairs).
   - Standing up: the guest's next move takes it out of the sit schedule on
     the host. To check in play that the pose shows on everyone (it should:
     nearby NPC moves carry the frame).
   - Chairs stay in Furniture (off by default), so E doesn't offer them; the
     double-click and the pick by mouse work regardless.

6. **The watch and abacus speak over whoever carries them.** Both scripts
   (watch 0x9F, abacus 675 frame 11) `item_say` on the item. For a thing in
   a pack with no window open, Exult puts the text over the carrier: over the
   Avatar when the Avatar holds the watch, over Iolo for his abacus. And since
   the last round, speech from a thing in a pack isn't sent at all
   (`Friend_mode_note_speech`, "says it only to whoever used it"), at the
   user's request; this round reverts that: the text looks like the player
   saying it, so everyone should see it.
   **Fix:**
   - In `item_say` (Friend Mode only): when the item is in a pack, the text
     goes over **the one who used it**: the guest's companion while the host
     runs a script for a guest (`Friend_mode_usecode_walker`), else the
     host's own (the Avatar, or the companion the host leads); on a guest
     running one locally, its own companion.
   - Host: that speech goes to guests as that actor's speech (the revert).
   - Guest: speech from a script the guest runs locally (the watch, the
     abacus, the coin, the sundial) goes to the host as a new **guest local
     speech** blob, which the host shows and passes to the other guests.
     It counts only for scripts started by `Friend_mode_guest_activate`'s
     local branch, the W key and the ring's Watch, and for their delayed
     steps (the coin's lines come later: `ucsched` already carries the
     walker; it carries this mark too).
   - Host checks (lobby-security rule: treat a guest's bytes as untrusted):
     the speaker is the guest's own companion or a party member, the text is
     at most 200 bytes of printable text, at most a few a second; otherwise
     dropped with a log line.
   - The watch and abacus stay local on the guest (so a guest can still use
     a watch anyone in the party carries, as now).

7. **The coin toss on a guest isn't seen by the host.** Gold's script
   (0x284) closes windows, then says "Call it." over the Avatar (-356: the
   guest's companion on a guest) and heads or tails over Iolo, by a die
   roll, through fn 0933 (a delayed bark). It runs on the guest only, because
   it closes windows (it would close the host's). Nothing of it is sent.
   **Fix:** the guest local speech of 6: the guest keeps running it (its own
   die roll, its own windows), and the lines go to the host and the other
   guests. Edge: a guest playing Iolo would have Iolo answer himself, as it
   does today; left as is.

## As built (2026-11-12)

- **1, 4:** `Friend_mode_guest_usable_shape` has the harpsichord (689),
  xylophone (690), harp (745), the mysterious craft (267) and the ferry's
  horn (888): the host runs the script for the guest (reach as for doors),
  its music goes to everyone. While the host runs a script for a guest's
  companion (`Friend_mode_walker_is_guest`), `set_camera` does nothing on
  the host: the horn's frame 1 points the camera at the Ferryman and the
  ferry's arrival (0x637) points it back, with -356 being the guest's
  companion there.
- **2:** lyre, lute and whistle moved from Loot to Usable. Guests: lyre and
  lute "use on self" (`Friend_mode_guest_item_use`), the whistle only frame
  0 (frame 1 stirs the wisps: the Avatar's).
- Space says **Play** on the instruments and the horn and **Inspect** on the
  craft (`Friend_use_word`), on the map and in one's own pack. The craft
  joins Usable (was decor).
- **5:** the chair (873) is in `Friend_mode_guest_usable_shape`; for it
  `Friend_mode_use_object_for_guest` doesn't run the script but gives the
  guest's companion a `Sit_schedule` on that chair ("Taken" if someone sits
  there; refused while it fights). A guest's accepted step sets a sitting
  companion back to waiting. Space says **Sit**. The Avatar's own sitting
  already leaves guests' companions alone: they wait (schedule 16) on the
  host, and the script's loop skips those.
- **6:** `Friend_mode_item_speaker` (in `item_say`, in a session): a carried
  thing speaks over the walker (the guest's companion the host runs a script
  for, or our own on a guest), else the player's controlled actor. On the
  host, speech from a carried thing goes out again, as the carrier's (the
  2026-11-08 change reverted).
- **6, 7:** new blob `guest_speech` (kind 39): on a guest, whatever is said
  while the walker is set (its local scripts and their later steps: the
  coin's lines come by `delayed_execute_usecode_array`) goes to the host as
  NPC number and text. The host takes only its companion, the Avatar or the
  party as speaker, printable ASCII up to 200 bytes, at most 6 lines in 2
  seconds; shows it and sends it to the others (`send_speech`'s new
  `except`). Things on the map (the sundial) still speak only locally.

the Avatar walked. `Game_window::start_actor` sets the whole party to
follow when the Avatar starts from a schedule other than follow or combat
(as after it sat or waited), and `get_leader_followers` does the same for a

every party member not fighting, waiting or loitering each time the leader
steps, and a seated one is none of those; its `Sit_schedule` then walked it
back. It now skips a guest's companion too.
