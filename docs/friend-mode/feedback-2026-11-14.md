## Causes found, with the fix

1. **Controller can't page through the companion picker.** The picker
   (`Select_friend_actor`) is a `MenuList`: the companion's card, the left
   and right arrows, Spectate. The pad reaches it as keys (D-pad: arrow
   keys, A: Return), but `MenuList` only moves Up and Down between its
   entries; Left and Right go to the card, which ignores them. So on a pad
   you'd have to go down to an arrow and press A, and nothing shows where
   you are.
   **Fix:** the card takes Left and Right and answers as the arrows do
   (previous, next companion). The card stays selected, so A picks the
   one shown. Same on the keyboard.

2. **Rudyom's Wand has no Use in the Target System.** Its script (0x303)
   asks "on what?" (`click_on_item`): on blackrock (914) the user blasts it
   (`set_to_attack` with the wand); on the Black Gate (305) it's the
   endgame (or Batlin's "Not yet, Avatar."); on anything else, a sound and
   lightning. It is Loot (771), so on the map E takes it. Carried, Space
   says Use, but on a guest that's "The Avatar must do this". In a party
   member's window there's no Use at all.
   **Fix:**
   - Space and the item menu say **Use** on the wand wherever it is; its
     crosshair has E aim at blackrock (like the flour's tables).
   - Guests: "use on a target", the host allowing only blackrock within
     the companion's reach (the -356 in `set_to_attack` is already the
     guest's companion while the host runs it). The Black Gate stays the
     Avatar's.

3. **Dropping Rudyom's Wand (guest, controller) went back into the pack,
   a few times, then worked.** No log from that session. A guest's drop is
   two requests (lift, then move); when the host refuses one, it resends
   the pack and the item reappears where it was. Likely causes: the wand
   readied in a hand, and the host's pack pass landing between the lift
   and the drop. **Plan:** check the readied-item path through the drop
   cursor in a test build, and make the host's refusals say which check
   failed (some are silent now). Then reproduce with the log.

4. **Eating on a guest uses only its own pack.** Food (F, the ring's Eat,
   `Game_window::activate_item`) looks in the guest's companion only, and
   the host checks the food is the guest's (`Friend_guest_may_touch`).
   Parties keep food on one member, so a companion without food can't eat.
   **Fix:** for food (377) and drinks (616) only, a guest may eat from any
   party member's pack (the Avatar's too), still eating it itself. The host
   checks that the food is in the party and the eater is the guest's own
   companion. This is a carve-out from "guests touch only their own".

5. **Guests don't see the Light spell.** Light and Great Light set
   `Game_window::special_light` (the game minute it ends) on the host and
   the palette brightens. Guests get the clock but not that. **Fix:** the
   clock blob carries `special_light` and `ambient_light` too; the guest
   sets both and refigures the palette when they change. Protocol version
   goes up.

6. **Caltrops in the Target System.** Caltrops (756) have no script and
   are already Loot (not Decor), so E offers them to take, if they can be
   taken. E skips loot that can't be picked up, so caltrops fixed in the
   map (a trap) aren't offered, as Exult wouldn't let anyone take them.
   **To check:** where the ones seen lay; if fixed in the map, what E
   should do with them (nothing, or show them as a hazard).

7. **"Straying from the Avatar" stacks on guests.** `Friend_mode_update_
   leash_zone` adds the notice every time the companion crosses from near
   to straying. Walking along the edge crosses it again and again, and the
   chat bubbles stack (three, plus "The Avatar needs you").
   **Fix:** a little slack (back under the line by 2 tiles before it counts
   as near again), at most one each 10 seconds, and a new one replaces the
   old one instead of stacking.

8. **Jaana's skill from the controller didn't heal; the keyboard did.** Both
   go to the same place (`Friend_class_use_slot`, run on the next frame), so
   it isn't a different path. Most likely the timing: her heals choose by
   need, and right then nobody needed it, or the bar said why (cooldown,
   "Wait for the Avatar"). **Plan:** say the reason as a notice, not only on
   the bar, when a skill used from the ring or a key does nothing. Log it
   too, so a repeat shows the cause.

## New features

a. **Food list.** Eat (F, the ring) opens a list of the party's food by
   kind, with how many ("Bread (3)", "Mutton (1)"), like the item menu's
   list (`Controller_choose`). Pick one: on the host the food's own
   crosshair asks who eats; on a guest its own companion eats (through 4).
   **Feasible, small:** the list and the eat paths exist.

b. **Drop or give all of a kind.** The item menu (Ctrl+Space, Cross held)
   gets **Give all ... to** and **Drop all ...** for a thing whose window
   holds more of the same kind (same shape: all the food, all the arrows).
   Drop all asks where once (the drop cursor), then drops each one there: on
   a party member, into their pack; on the floor, beside each other.
   **Feasible:**
   - Host and single player: small; a loop over what's in that window.
   - Guest: Give isn't offered to guests today (the Avatar's), but Drop is.
     A guest's drop is two requests per item through the host, so dropping
     many in a row wants one request for all of them ("drop these, there"),
     checked by the host as one drop. Medium. Also the cleanest way to rule
     out the bounce in 3 for this case.
   - Decided: "same kind" is the exact same item, shape and frame (all the
     bread, not all the food), for now.

## As built (2026-11-14)

- **1:** the picker's card (`Friend_actor_card::handle_event`) answers Left
  and Right as the arrows (-2, -3), so the pad's D-pad pages and A picks.
- **4:** `Friend_mode_party_food`: food (377) or drink (616) in the Avatar's
  or a party member's pack. On a guest, `activate_item` (F, the ring's Eat)
  falls back to that; the host lets the guest's own companion eat it
  (`Friend_mode_use_item_for_guest`, "use on self" only).
- **5:** the clock blob carries the Light spell's end (game minute) and the
  permanent light; the guest sets them (`Game_window::set_network_light`)
  and refigures the palette when they change. It ends on the guest as on
  the host, the clocks being one.
- **7:** `Friend_mode_update_leash_zone`: 2 tiles of slack back out of a
  zone, and each notice at most once each 10 seconds.
- **2:** Rudyom's Wand is "use on a target" for guests (`Friend_craft_target`
  771 -> 914), the host checking blackrock within reach (the script doesn't
  walk); the Black Gate (305) is refused, so the endgame stays the Avatar's.
  As a craft thing it's usable on the map (Space: Use, Alt+Space takes); in
  one's own pack Space was already Use; in a party member's window Space now
  says Use too (`Friend_is_wand`). In its crosshair E offers blackrock
  already (Loot, can be taken).
- **3:** the host already logged each refused guest move with a reason. The
  likely one: a drop on the floor more than 8 tiles from the companion
  ("drop spot out of reach"); the pad's drop cursor can aim that far, the
  guest's copy drops it, the host refuses, and the pack is sent again. Now
  the guest's drop cursor says "Too far away to drop there" first, and every
  refusal (lift or move) sends the guest a notice of why
  (`Friend_mode_tell_guest`). The lift's log names its reason too.
- **8:** `Friend_class_use_slot` (the ring, keys) leaves the slot for the
  next frame, which dropped it without a word if the game was waiting
  (paused, or a live pass), and the ring's pick comes as it closes. Now it
  waits up to 1.5 seconds, then says "Not now: the game is waiting" (and
  logs it). Skills that can't be used already said why.

## Features built and tested (2026-11-15)

- **a, food list:** `Friend_mode_choose_food` (F through `ActionUseFood`,
  and the ring's Eat, which fed the hungriest with no crosshair before):
  the party's food (377) and drink (616) grouped by shape and frame, with
  totals, one's own first; one kind skips the list. The host then gets the
  food's own crosshair (who eats); a guest runs `Friend_mode_guest_activate`,
  its companion eating it (from anyone's pack, item 4).
- **b, Give all / Drop all:** in the item menu for a thing in a party
  window that holds more of the very same item (`Friend_same_items`: same
  shape and frame, same container). Give all (not guests) asks the member
  once and moves each whole; Drop all asks where once (the drop cursor,
  `Friend_mode_drop_where`'s new `more`) and drops each there. A guest's are
  one lift and one drop per item, in order, through the host as before.

## Follow-ups (2026-11-15), tested

- **Who eats:** after the food, the host gets a list of the party (those who
  eat), "Auto (the hungriest)" first, instead of the food's crosshair; the
  pick answers the script's click (`Friend_mode_choose_food`). A guest still
  feeds its own companion.
- **W and S** in every `Controller_choose` list (orders, food, the item
  menu, Give): `Choice_gump::key_down` turns them into Up and Down.
- **The hint:** the three actions as before when the item menu has nothing
  else; when it has more (Give, Split, Drop all...), Space's and
  Shift+Space's and "More (Ctrl+Space)", on a pad "More (hold <button>)".
  The hint asks the menu itself (`Friend_item_menu_choices`, by label), so
  they can't disagree. First try showed More on a light source whose menu
  was only Use and Take, and the pad said Ctrl+Space: the item menu is a
  held button, and the label lookup knew only tapped ones
  (`Controller_hold_label`). "Auto (the hungriest)" is "Auto (hungriest)".
- Second look: a cup in one's own pack offered Drop twice (Space's action
  and the menu's own Drop); the menu now has each entry once. The abacus in
  another party member's window only said Take: there, a thing with a use
  (not worn, not a bag) now says Use on Space and Take on Shift+Space
  (`Friend_window_use`; was the wand only).
- No Take in a party member's window (2026-11-15): it is all the player's
  own, so moving a thing from one companion to another is Give. There Space
  uses, reads, readies or opens, and on a plain thing gives; Shift+Space
  gives; Alt+Space puts some of a worn stack away. The item menu drops Take
  and Split there.
- Then, the rule for the party's packs (2026-11-15): they are all the
  Avatar's inventory, one's own pack or (the host) any party member's. A
  thing there has its actions in order (`Friend_inventory_actions`): its
  own (Read, Use, Pour Flour, Open, Ready or Put away), Drop, Split (a
  stack: put some away when worn, else drop some), Give (not guests), then
  Drop all and Give all. Space, Shift+Space and Alt+Space are the first
  three; the hint shows all of them when three or fewer, else the first two
  and More; the item menu lists them all. A cup: "Drop, Give"; 30 bolts:
  "Ready, Drop, More" (More: Split, Give); the abacus: "Use, Drop, Give".
