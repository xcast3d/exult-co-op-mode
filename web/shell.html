To play, you need your own copy of Ultima VII: The Black Gate (from GOG, Steam, or the original disks). Choose the folder where it is installed.

◆ Your game files are copied into this browser's private storage. Nothing is uploaded.

Phones aren't supported. The game needs a keyboard and a mouse or trackpad, or a game controller, and its text is too small for a phone's screen.

On a tablet, connect a keyboard, mouse or controller, then press any key or controller button to go on.
